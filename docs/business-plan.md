# Business Plan – RepairFlow CRM

## Pricing
- **Monthly Subscription**: ₹299/shop/month (basic), ₹599 (pro with inventory).
- **One‑time Purchase**: ₹2,999 (1 year updates, own hosting).

## Why Would Someone Buy?
- Saves 2–3 hours/day of manual record‑keeping.
- Increases repeat customers through loyalty features.
- Professional job sheets & bills build trust.
- Cheaper than existing CRMs (which start at ₹800/month).

## Biggest Competitor
- **RepairDesk** (cloud‑based, expensive, complex).
- **Local Excel sheets** (free but error‑prone).

## Biggest Weakness
- No cloud backup – data lost if laptop crashes. (Will be solved in v5 with Firebase sync.)

## Feature That Would Make Them Switch
- **"Customer Trust Timeline"** – customers can scan a QR code and see live repair status on their phone without calling the shop. This transparency is unique in small shops.
